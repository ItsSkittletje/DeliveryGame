# Copyright Policy

Binding rules are in `MasterPrompt.md` §14. Operational procedure:

1. **Before importing any asset** (model, texture, material, sound, music, font, plugin, code snippet), record it in `AssetLicenses.md` or `ThirdPartySoftware.md` with source URL and license. No entry, no import.
2. **Unclear license = not used** until reviewed.
3. **Never** extract assets from existing games, films or online videos.
4. **AI-generated assets**: record the tool, its commercial-use terms, and the prompt in `AssetLicenses.md`; review output for resemblance to existing IP before use.
5. **Original creative elements** (characters, entities, house designs, story beats, names) are logged in `OriginalityRegister.md` with date and a short note on how they differ from cited inspirations.
6. **Inspiration ≠ implementation.** The Conjuring (atmosphere) and Phasmophobia (co-op investigation loop) are genre references only. No layouts, entities, equipment, UI, scenes, dialogue or branding are reproduced.
7. **Pre-release**: trademark/title/domain clearance must be done with a qualified professional. An unused Steam title or domain is not proof of availability.

Note: this policy is a development practice, not legal advice.
